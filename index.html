RSI(14), Wilder smoothing, on MEXC USDT perpetual candles. The candle still forming is included, so the value moves with the live price and can cross back over the line before the candle closes. A coin needs at least 20 candles on a timeframe to get a value there, so recent listings show "–" on 1W.

Rows are coins past the chosen level on the chosen timeframe; every RSI cell is coloured, so you can see at a glance where the other timeframes agree. "3/5" means past the level on 3 of the 5 timeframes. Data from MEXC's public API, refreshed about every 10 minutes; this page checks for new data every minute. An extreme RSI means a strong recent run or slide, not a signal on its own.

Dip in uptrend = a short, sharp drop inside a rising coin: on 4H candles EMA50 is above EMA200 and the price is still above EMA200 (shown as ↑ and the % above EMA200 in "4H trend"), daily RSI is between 40 and 65 (trend healthy, not overheated), and 15m RSI is at or below the chosen level (30 / 25 / 20). Rows tagged "Dip ↑" in the other views meet it at 30. This setup has not yet been tested on past MEXC data; treat it as a watchlist, not a buy signal.
